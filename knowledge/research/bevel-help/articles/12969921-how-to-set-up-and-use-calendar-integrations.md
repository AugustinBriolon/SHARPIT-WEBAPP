# How to Set Up and Use Calendar Integrations

**Category:** Bevel Intelligence  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/12969921

Connect your Google Calendar or iCloud Calendar to Bevel Intelligence so it can read your events and better understand your schedule. Note: Bevel currently does not support writing to calendar events. Connect a Calendar Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Bevel Intelligence. Tap Connect Google Calendar or Connect iCloud Calendar. Connect iCloud Calendar Bevel requires an app-specific password to connect to your iCloud Calendar. Sign in to your Apple Account. Go to App-Specific Passwords. Generate a new app-specific password for Bevel. Copy the generated password. Return to Bevel and enter your Apple ID and the app-specific password to connect your calendar. Connect Google Calendar Sign in to your Google account. Review the requested permissions and, if you agree, allow Bevel to access your calendar. Manage Connected Calendars You can choose which calendars Bevel Intelligence can view, including calendars you've subscribed to. Open Customization → Bevel Intelligence. Under Accounts, tap the username of your connected email account. Under Connected Calendars, toggle individual calendars on or off to control which calendars Bevel Intelligence can access. Disconnect a Calendar Open Customization → Bevel Intelligence. Under Accounts, tap the username of your connected email account. Scroll to the bottom of the page. Tap Disconnect to remove the calendar integration. Is my calendar data secure? Yes. Your calendar data is protected using industry-standard security practices. Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and strict access controls are in place to limit who can access it. Before any calendar data is sent to our large language model (LLM) providers, it is anonymized to remove identifying information. Other Bevel users cannot see your calendar, and access to your data is restricted to authorized systems and personnel only when necessary to operate the service.
