# How To Connect Devices

**Category:** Data Sources  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10400449

Bevel lets you connect supported devices to automatically sync your data. Connecting devices ensures your metrics, Trends, and Insights are accurate and up to date. If a device uploads data to Apple Health, Bevel can also pull those Apple Health metrics automatically. Steps to Connect a Device Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Data Sources Tap the + button next to Connect Account. Select your device. Follow the on-screen pairing prompts for that device. Once connected, your data will automatically sync to Bevel. Supported Devices & Integration Limitations Below is a list of supported devices and known limitations with each integration. These limitations affect which data can be synced into Bevel and may vary by platform and data type. Device Integration Limitations Apple Watch Full integration with Apple Health, providing seamless access to Sleep, Fitness and daily metrics. None Garmin Watch & other devices Direct integration with Garmin Connect™ to read all Sleep, Fitness and daily metrics. Limited to 1 month of historical data No daytime HRV Stress & Energy are estimated from heart rate only (may be less precise). Garmin-only workouts will sync (i.e. no third-party workouts from Zwift or Rouvy) No Garmin Connect+ Strength or Nutrition data Edits made in Garmin Connect do not sync back to Bevel Running metrics such as vertical oscillation, stride length, and ground contact time Oura Ring Direct integration with Oura to read all Sleep, Fitness and daily metrics. No daytime HRV Stress & Energy are estimated from heart rate only (may be less precise). Requires an active subscription with Oura Requires Oura to write to Apple Health for sleep and workout route data Running metrics such as vertical oscillation, stride length, and ground contact time Google Health Direct integration with Google Health to read all Sleep, Fitness, and daily metrics. If you use Fitbit, your Fitbit data can also sync to Bevel through Google Health. Google Health includes an additional sleep stage called Restlessness. Bevel does not display Restlessness as a separate sleep stage. Instead, periods marked as Restlessness are included in REM sleep. Resting calories are not available until the end of the day. Workouts from Strava will not be imported to Bevel. Strava workouts can be written to Apple Health but will be missing heart rate data. Amazfit Helio Strap (Zepp App) Integration through Apple Health to read all Sleep, Fitness and daily metrics.
