# How to Purchase Additional Bevel Intelligence Credits

**Category:** Bevel Intelligence  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/12741505

If you reach your weekly Bevel Intelligence credit limit, you can purchase additional credits at any time to continue using Bevel Intelligence features. Unused purchased credits roll over week to week and never expire. Purchase Additional Credits Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Bevel Intelligence. Under Usage and Extra Credits, tap Manage Credits. Under Purchase Additional Credits, choose a credit pack and tap Buy __ Credits. Follow the checkout steps to complete your purchase. Actual credit consumption may vary based on the complexity of the request and the amount of data processed. *Note: Standard Bevel features are free to use and do not require credits. This includes Capture Food, Import Food, Describe Food, and generating workout Routines. Credits are only used when you access features within Bevel Intelligence (BI), such as BI-powered food logging or workout generation.
