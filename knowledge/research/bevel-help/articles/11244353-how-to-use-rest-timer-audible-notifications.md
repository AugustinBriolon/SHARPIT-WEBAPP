# How to Use Rest Timer Audible Notifications

**Category:** Strength Builder  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11244353

Bevel provides an audible notification for your Workout rest timers, so you know exactly when your rest period is over. By default, your Apple Watch may be silenced, which means you won’t hear the sound.  How to Enable Rest Timer Audible Notifications: Open Settings on your Apple Watch. Tap Sounds & Haptics. Make sure Silent Mode is off. Adjust Alert Volume to your preferred level. Adjust Haptic Strength as desired. This ensures you never miss a rest period, even during intense workouts. Note: This feature currently does not work with headphones.
