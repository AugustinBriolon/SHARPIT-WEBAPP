# How to Upload Health Records

**Category:** Health Records  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11587265

There are multiple ways you can add Health Records to Bevel, making it easy to upload files from your device whenever you receive new information. Upload Health Records Option 1: Via Bevel Intelligence Sidebar Open Bevel Intelligence. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner. Tap Health Records. Tap the + button. Tap Add Records. Choose your upload type (Photo, Library, or File) and follow the on-screen instructions. Option 2: Via Bevel Intelligence Chat Open Bevel Intelligence. Attach your test results and ask Bevel Intelligence to add it to your Health Records. Option 3: Via Biology Upload Blood Tests Open Bevel. Tap the Biology tab. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Tap Upload Blood Tests. Follow steps below to Upload from Phone or Desktop. Option 4: Via Biology View Documents Open Bevel. Tap the Biology tab. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Tap View Health Records. Tap the + button. Tap Add Records. Choose your upload type (Photo, Library, or File) and follow the on-screen instructions. Option 5: Via Biology, no Biological Age Open Bevel. Tap the Biology tab. Tap Add Blood Tests. Follow steps below to Upload from Phone or Desktop. Option 6: Via Individual Blood Biomarkers Open Bevel. Tap the Biology tab. Tap a Blood Test (such as Albumin). Tap the refresh icon in the top-right corner. Tap the + button in the top-right corner. Follow steps below to Upload from Phone or Desktop. Option 7: Via Blood Biomarkers Open Bevel. Tap the Biology tab. Tap Confidence beneath your Biological Age. Tap + next to Blood Biomarkers. Upload Documents from Desktop You can upload tests directly from a desktop computer. Tap Upload from Desktop. Tap Copy URL or Send Via… Open URL on your Desktop. Sign In with Email or Apple ID Tap Select Files or + Add Files. Choose the files you want to add. Tap Upload Files.
