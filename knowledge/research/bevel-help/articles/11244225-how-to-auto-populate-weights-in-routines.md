# How to Auto-Populate Weights in Routines

**Category:** Strength Builder  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11244225

Bevel can automatically carry over your weights and reps from your last workout into your workout Routine How to Update Routine with Weights: Complete your workout as usual. At the end of the workout, look for the “Update Routine” button on the device you finished the workout on (phone or watch). Tap “Update Routine” to save the weights and reps you just performed into that specific Routine. This ensures the next time you use the Routine, your recent values are pre-filled.  Note: these updates do not carry over to other Routines. Tip: On Apple Watch, this button is easy to miss. You’ll need to scroll past the workout summary to see it. Once updated, the next time you use that Routine, your previous weights and reps will already be filled in. Note: You can enable Auto-Update Routine in Settings to automatically update your workout templates with your latest weights after each workout.
