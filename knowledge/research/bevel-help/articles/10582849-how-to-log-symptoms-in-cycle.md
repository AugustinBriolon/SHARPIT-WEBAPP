# How to Log Symptoms in Cycle

**Category:** Cycle  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10582849

Logging symptoms helps Bevel better understand how your body changes throughout your cycle and provides more personalized Insights. These symptoms also sync with Apple Health, meaning they can both read from and write to Apple Health. Steps to Log Symptoms Option 1: Open Bevel. Tap Current Cycle under the Cycle section. Tap the + button below and to the right of today’s date. Tap Add Symptoms or the + button under Symptoms. Choose your symptom(s). Follow the on-screen prompts if a symptom requires additional details (e.g., mild, severe). If prompted, tap Add Symptom to save that entry. To add all selected symptoms, tap Add in the bottom-right corner. Tap Add to Log. Option 2: Open Bevel. Tap Current Cycle under the Cycle section. Tap underneath today’s date. Tap + Symptom in the bottom-left corner. Choose your symptom(s). Follow the on-screen prompts if a symptom requires additional details (e.g., mild, severe). If prompted, tap Add Symptom to save that entry. To add all selected symptoms, tap Add in the bottom-right corner. Tap Add to Log. Available Symptoms Bleeding Patterns: Spotting Stomach and Abdominal: Abdominal Cramps, Bloating, Constipation, Diarrhea, Nausea Constitutional: Appetite Changes, Chills, Fatigue, Hot Flashes Musculoskeletal: Lower Back Pain Neurological: Headache, Memory Lapse, Mood Changes Reproduction: Breast Pain, Pelvic Pain, Dryness Skin & Hair: Acne, Dry Skin, Hair Loss Sleep: Night Sweats, Sleep Changes Urinary: Bladder Incontinence
