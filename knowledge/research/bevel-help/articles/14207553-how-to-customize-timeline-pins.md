# How to Customize Timeline Pins

**Category:** Timeline  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/14207553

The pins at the top of Timeline are fully customizable. You can add, remove, reorder, and change how each pinned metric is displayed. How to customize your pins Option 1: Via Pinned Metrics Open Bevel. Scroll down to Timeline. Tap View Timeline. Tap Add to the right of your existing pins. Option 2: Via the three-dot menu Open Bevel. Scroll down to Timeline. Tap View Timeline. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Tap Edit Pins. From either option, you can: Pin a metric: Tap the pin icon next to an unpinned metric. Unpin a metric: Tap the minus button next to a pinned metric. Reorder your pins: Press and hold the three-line icon, then drag the metric to your desired position. Customize how metrics are displayed Each pinned metric can show either the amount Remaining or Consumed, displayed as a Value or Percentage. To change these settings: Open your full Timeline. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner. Tap Display Options. Select your preferred display option for each metric. For example, if you have consumed 500 calories and your daily goal is 2,000 calories: Remaining Value: 1,500 left Consumed Value: 500 / 2,000 Remaining Percentage: 75% left Consumed Percentage: 25% Tap any pin to view more information about the metric and what affected it. Note: You must have a goal set for a metric before goal-based values can be displayed. See How to Track Nutrition Goals in Bevel for instructions.
