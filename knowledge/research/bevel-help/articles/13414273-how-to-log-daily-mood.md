# How to Log Daily Mood

**Category:** Journal  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/13414273

This article explains how to log your Daily Mood in Journal.  Your most recent Daily Mood entry is categorized in Insights as either a Pleasant Mood or Unpleasant Mood. How to log a Daily Mood entry Open Bevel. Tap the Journal tab. Tap Daily Mood. Tap Add Historical. Use the slider to rate how you feel: Very Unpleasant Unpleasant Neutral Pleasant Very Pleasant Tap Add to Journal, or tap Add Details for more context. Add Details (optional) You can add emotions and what may be contributing to your mood. Choose between Positive or Negative emotions. Select as many emotions as you'd like. Tap Continue or Add to Journal. Positive emotions Amazed Amused Brave Calm Confident Content Excited Grateful Happy Hopeful Joyful Passionate Peaceful Proud Relieved Satisfied Surprised Negative emotions Angry Annoyed Anxious Ashamed Disappointed Discouraged Disgusted Drained Embarrassed Frustrated Guilty Hopeless Indifferent Irritated Jealous Lonely Overwhelmed Sad Scared Stressed Worried If you tapped Continue, select what may be contributing to your mood. You can choose multiple options: Community Current Events Dating Education Family Fitness Friends Health Hobbies Identity Money Partner Self-care Spirituality Tasks Travel Weather Work Tap Add to Journal. How to edit or delete a Daily Mood entry Open Journal → Daily Mood. Tap the entry you want to change. You can: Tap Edit Log to update the entry. Tap the red trash icon to delete it. Daily Mood settings Open Journal → Daily Mood, then tap the Settings icon in the top-right corner. From here you can: Pin Daily Mood Tap the Pin icon to pin Daily Mood to the top of your Journal. Enable Nighttime Entry Turn on Nighttime Entry (Log yesterday's entry on wake up) if you prefer to log your Daily Mood the following morning instead of the night before. Tap Save to apply your changes.
