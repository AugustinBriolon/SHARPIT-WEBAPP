# Request a Feature

**Category:** Contact and Support  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/2126337

Give Our App a Healthy Upgrade! You can request a feature by visiting our public roadmap and tapping the "Feature Request" button. Alternatively, you can request a feature in-app by going to Profile > Request a feature.
