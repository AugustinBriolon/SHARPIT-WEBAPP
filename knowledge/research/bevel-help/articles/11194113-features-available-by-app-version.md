# Features Available by App Version

**Category:** Bevel Basics  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11194113

A list of features available on each app version in the Free and Pro versions of Bevel. This is a high-level overview. You can find our detailed major Release Notes here, and minor updates in the App Store changelogs. Pro Features Bevel Intelligence as of 12/18/2025 Biological Age Health Records Versions 3.2.0 and above Fitness: Redesigned Fitness around daily activity, Cardio Load, Muscular Load, Routines, and strength and cardio progress. Training Calendar: Plan and manage activities, review workout history, and filter by metric or activity type. Bevel Intelligence Training Plans: Create training plans with Bevel Intelligence and review, schedule, or migrate plans and scheduled activities in Training Calendar. Muscular Load & Muscle Freshness: Muscular Load tracks the load placed on individual muscles by strength and cardio activities. Muscle Freshness shows how recovered each muscle is. Routines: Templates have been renamed to Routines. Strength and Cardio Routines now live together and can be created, edited, scheduled on Training Calendar, sent to Apple Watch, or started through Bevel Intelligence. Planned Activities: Completed activities started with Strength Builder or a Cardio Routine on Apple Watch automatically link to their corresponding planned activity. Activity Charts: Track duration, distance, elevation, energy, pace, speed, heart rate, power, and cadence over time. Heart Rate Zones: Track time spent in each heart rate zone over time. Strength Progression: Track 1RM, Heaviest Weight, Volume, Sets, and Reps by exercise, plus Volume, Sets, Muscular Load, and Muscle Freshness by muscle group. Activity Details: View workout and interval breakdowns, Muscular Load, running-form metrics including vertical oscillation, stride length, and ground contact time, and multisport breakdowns with transitions. Personal Records: Track Strength records including Heaviest Weight, Estimated 1 Rep Max, Session Volume, Set Volume, and Set Reps, and Cardio records for running, cycling, and swimming. Fitness Customization: Reorder or hide Fitness sections and pin favorite activity, exercise, muscle, and cardio charts. Strength Builder: Added 200+ exercises to the exercise library. Timeline: View activities, sleep, food, and other daily events in a chronological timeline. Food Logging: Copy foods across dates, edit date and time, create recipes, select multiple foods, delete logs, and log foods for future dates. Timeline Pins: Pin favorite scores, macros, and micronutrients, with options to display nutrient values by percentage or weight and view remaining or consumed amounts. Bevel Intelligence: Improved workout context with full workout details, improved overnight and long-term memory, added text selection and copying for your own messages, and improved response speed. Nutrition Score: Vegetable Contributor scoring now uses a daily 150g target instead of calorie contribution. 3.1.12 and above Added support for native Recovery HRV (RMSSD) data from Apple Health 3.1.11 and above Nutrients: Data Sources → Nutrition has been renamed to Nutrients to clarify that this setting controls nutrient amounts, not food logs or Nutrition Score. 3.1.10 and above Added support for iOS 27 3.1.8 and above Blood Pressure Data Source: Added a Data Source setting for Blood Pressure to show or hide data from individual sources Split up Nutrition Goals by macros, target and limit nutrients in Settings → Customization → Goals → Nutrition Strain Performance: Added Strain Performance card to Strain Dashboard 3.1.4 and above Connect an External Heart Rate Sensor: Select a Bluetooth heart rate sensor, such as Fitbit Air, for iPhone-only strength workouts. 3.1.3 and above Bevel Intelligence Advanced Settings: Opt into an upgraded model and beta features. 3.1.0 and above Google Health Integration Blood Pressure: Add Blood Pressure to Biology, with the ability to log directly in Bevel or read from Apple Health. Nutrition Database Update: Add thousands of new foods to the Nutrition Database, with more being added daily. 12-Month Commitment Monthly Plan: Add a 12-month commitment monthly subscription plan, allowing eligible members to commit to a year of Bevel while paying monthly. Not currently available for users in the US or Singapore. Strength Builder Updates: Live Sync update, AirPods Pro 3 Heart Rate Support, Auto Update Template, Watch exercise previews, Watch set and rep editing, Autofill sets, Plate Calculator updates Bevel Intelligence Updates: Cardio Workout Templates, Google and iCloud Calendar integrations, Ghost Mode, Thinking Modes 3.0.9 and above Bevel Intelligence Credits: Add the ability to purchase extra Bevel Intelligence credits from Settings and when you hit usage limits. 3.0.8 and above Stress Score: Improve the Stress Score algorithm. Late Meal Journal Tag: Add a calorie threshold to the late meal Journal tag, so low-calorie entries like water or supplements won't count as late meals. 3.0.6 and above Manual Check-ins: Create, view, edit, and manage check-ins manually in Bevel Intelligence. Supports one-time, hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly frequencies. Edit Alcohol: Edit default alcohol intake and log alcoholic drinks directly from the alcohol chart in Biology. 30-Day Projection for Body Fat Percentage and Lean Body Mass 3.0.3 and above New Languages: Portuguese (Brazil), Polish, and Russian Social Sharing: Share Biological Age and Age Projection 3.0.0 and above Bevel Intelligence V2: More powerful & capable. Includes coaching personalities, proactive check-ins, a new Files system, artifact creation, conversational food logging, and AI-powered training plans and strength workouts. Biological Age: A long-term health metric powered by your wearable data, daily habits, and bloodwork, recalculated weekly. Health Records: Upload blood tests and health documents directly into Bevel, and view blood biomarkers in Biology. 2.5.7 and above Social Sharing: Share main metrics or activities in multiple styles with customization. Referrals: Refer your friends to get Bevel Pro and gain exclusive rewards. 2.5.0 and above Strength Builder: Plate Calculator, Rest Timers, Live Activities, Progression History Watch App: Redesigned app with more Nutrition and training metrics Oura & Strava integrations 2.4.0 and above All features except Bevel Intelligence became free 2.3.0 and above Cycle Tracking: Sync with Apple Health, log flow and symptoms, phase predictions & insights. New Nutrition Score: Focused on food quality throughout the day, not individual food macros. Garmin Connect™ Integration Camera Control (iPhone 15+) for logging foods 2.2.0 and above Bevel Intelligence Nutrition: Goal tracking, Custom Foods, Recipe Import Strength Builder: Live Sync, Supersets & other set types, video instructions, redesigned watch app. Action Button: + button with custom actions. 2.1.0 and above Fitness tab: New charts for Activity Summary, Strain Performance, Cardio Load, Cardio Focus, Heart Rate Recovery and Strength Progression
