# How to Use the Fitness Tab

**Category:** Fitness  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/14206081

The Fitness Tab brings your weekly fitness data together in one place, with sections for Today’s Activity, Routines, Training Load, Cardio, and Strength. Navigate Between Days You can move between days using any of the following options: Tap a day in the weekly view. Swipe left or right to move between weeks. Tap the calendar button in the top-left corner, then select a date from the Training Calendar. Swipe up or down to move between months. A list of the activities matching your selected filters appears below the calendar. View the Calendar by Metric You can change which metric appears on the monthly calendar: Open the Training Calendar. Tap By Metric in the top-right corner. Select one of the following metrics: Distance Active Duration Elevation Gain Active Energy Note: Metric filters apply to completed activities only. Planned activities will not be included. Filter the Calendar by Activity You can display one or more activity types on your calendar: Open the Training Calendar. Tap the filter icon with three lines in the top-right corner. Select one or more activities. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your selections. The calendar and activity list will update based on your selected filters.
