# Understanding Energy Bank (Dotted Line)

**Category:** Stress and Energy  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10581377

If you don’t wear your watch, a dotted line appears in your Energy Bank chart. This line predicts your Energy levels based on your historical patterns and connects the dots when new data is available. The predicted Energy will gradually decrease over time. To get accurate, real-time Energy tracking again, wear your watch for at least 2 days, and the predictions will reset.
