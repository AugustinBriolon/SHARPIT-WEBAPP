# How to Edit the Home Tab

**Category:** App Customization  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10417089

Bevel allows you to customize what appears on your Home Tab by adjusting the visibility and order of individual sections. This makes it easy to tailor the Home experience to what matters most to you. This article explains how to edit, hide, show, reorder, and reset Home Tab sections. Edit Home Tab To access Home Tab editing: Open Bevel. Navigate to the Home Tab. Scroll to the bottom. Tap Edit Home. You’ll now see a list representing your current Home Tab layout. Hiding Sections To hide a section from the Home Tab: From Edit Home, locate the section you want to hide. Tap the negative (–) button in the top-left corner of the section. Tap Save. The section will no longer appear on your Home Tab. Showing Sections To add a section back to the Home Tab: From Edit Home, scroll down to Add card. Tap Add card. Select the section you want to add. Tap Add to home. Tap Save. The section will now be visible on your Home Tab. Reordering Home Tab Sections You can change the order in which sections appear on your Home Tab. To reorder sections: From Edit Home, press and hold the section you want to move. Drag it to your desired position. Release to place it. Tap Save. Your Home Tab will update to reflect the new order. Restoring Default Visibility If you want to return to Bevel’s default Home Tab layout: From Edit Home, scroll down to Reset to default. Tap Reset to default. Tap Save. This will restore the original Home Tab sections and order. Note: If a section does not appear while editing your Home Tab, make sure it is enabled in Customization. To Enable/Disable a Section via Customization Open Bevel. Navigate to the Home Tab. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. In Bevel Intelligence, Nutrition, and Cycle, you can enable or disable a section, which also affects its related features and UI.  Remove a Section from Within Its Feature: For Nutrition and Cycle, you can disable a section directly from within that feature: Open the section you want to remove. For Nutrition: tap the three dots, then the gear icon in the top-right corner. For Cycle: tap the gear icon in the top-right corner. Change Visibility to disable the section, removing all associated features and UI. Once disabled, the section and its features are fully hidden. You can re-enable it later by returning to the Customization. Customize Bevel to fit your workflow and make the Home Tab work the way you do. Plus, your Bevel Home Tab will now be mirrored on the Apple Watch app, so you can check your key metrics and Trends right from your wrist.
