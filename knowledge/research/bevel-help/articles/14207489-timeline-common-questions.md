# Timeline Common Questions

**Category:** Timeline  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/14207489

We’ve compiled answers to the most frequently asked questions about Timeline. Why don’t my Timeline Nutrition and Journal entries match? This usually happens when Nutrition and Journal are using different data sources. Timeline displays nutrition data logged directly in Bevel and data synced through Apple Health. Journal displays data from the source selected in your Journal settings. Step 1: Check your Nutrition settings Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Nutrition. Scroll down and make sure Write to Apple Health is toggled on. Step 2: Check your Journal settings Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Journal. Set the data source to Apple Health + Nutrition. Note: If these settings are configured differently, the values shown in Timeline and Journal may not match. How can I use Timeline to plan my meals ahead of time? You can plan your meals in advance by logging food for a future date. Simply select the date you want to plan for and log your meals as you normally would.
