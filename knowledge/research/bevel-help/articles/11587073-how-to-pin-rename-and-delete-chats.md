# How to Pin, Rename, and Delete Chats

**Category:** Bevel Intelligence  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11587073

You can pin conversations with Bevel Intelligence to easily return to them later, rename chats for better organization, or delete them when no longer needed. Pin, Rename, or Delete Chats Open Bevel Intelligence. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner. Press and hold the chat you want to manage. Select Pin Chat, Rename, or Delete Chat.
