# How to Use the Cardio Section

**Category:** Fitness  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/14206785

The Cardio section lets you customize which Activity and Cardio charts appear on your Fitness Tab. You can select the activities, metrics, chart types, and time periods that are most meaningful to you. Customize the Cardio Section To add or remove charts: Open Bevel. Go to the Fitness Tab. Scroll down to Cardio. Tap Edit next to the Cardio heading. Make your desired changes: Remove a chart: Tap the minus (–) button in the top-left corner of the chart. Add a chart: Tap Add Chart below your pinned charts, then select Activity Chart or Cardio Chart and customize it. Change Chart Layout: Drag and drop to choose the chart layout you want. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes. Activity Charts Activity Charts let you track selected metrics for a specific activity. When creating an Activity Chart: Select the activity you want to track. Select an available metric, such as: Active Duration Distance Elevation Gain Active Energy Pace Heart Rate If available, select how you want the chart displayed: Segmented: Displays the data as a bar chart. Cumulative: Displays the data as a cumulative line chart. Select a Daily or Weekly aggregation for the last 30 days. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your selections. Customize or Pin an Activity Chart After opening an Activity Chart, you can customize the following: Activity: Tap the current activity, then select a different activity. Metric: Select a different metric on the chart page. Aggregation: Select Daily or Weekly, when available. Chart Type: Tap the bar chart or line graph icon in the top-right corner. For additional chart settings: Tap the chart icon at the bottom of the chart. Adjust any of the following: Selected Date: Choose the date you want to view. Lookback: Change how far back the chart displays data. Display By: Display the data by Day, Week, or Month. Preferences: Choose whether to display your Activity Status. To save the chart to your Fitness Tab: Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Tap Pin Chart. Your corresponding activity history appears underneath the chart. Available Activity Metrics The available metrics depend on the selected activity: Activity Available metrics Running Active Duration, Distance, Elevation Gain, Active Energy, Avg Pace, Avg HR, Avg Power, Avg Cadence Cycling Active Duration, Distance, Elevation Gain, Active Energy, Avg Speed, Avg HR, Avg Power, Avg Cadence Swimming Active Duration, Distance, Active Energy, Avg Pace, Avg HR, Avg Cadence Rowing Active Duration, Distance, Active Energy, Avg Speed, Avg HR, Avg Cadence Other Activity Varies based on the data recorded. Personal Records When you open an Activity Chart from the Fitness Tab, your Personal Records appear underneath the chart when available. Swipe left or right to view other Personal Records for the activity. Tap an individual result to open the workout from that day. Cardio Charts Cardio Charts let you track the following cardiovascular metrics: VO₂ Max: Your estimated cardio fitness over time. Heart Rate Recovery: How your heart rate recovers after each qualifying workout. Cardio Focus: The balance of your aerobic and anaerobic training. Heart Rate Zones: The time spent in each heart rate zone during recorded activities. For supported charts, select a Daily or Weekly aggregation based on the last 30 days. After opening a Cardio Chart from the Fitness Tab, use the activity filters at the top to select which activities are included. Note: Heart Rate Zones only include data from recorded activities. Passive heart rate data is not included.
