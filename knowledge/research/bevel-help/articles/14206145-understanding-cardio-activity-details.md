# Understanding Cardio Activity Details

**Category:** Fitness  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/14206145

Cardio Activity Details provides a complete breakdown of a recorded cardio workout. The information available depends on the activity, recording device, and data collected during the workout. View Cardio Activity Details Option 1: Via Timeline Open Bevel. Scroll down to Timeline. Tap View Timeline. Tap the cardio activity you want to view. Option 2: Via the Fitness Tab Open Bevel. Go to the Fitness Tab. Scroll down to Today’s Activity. Tap the cardio activity you want to view. Activity Summary At the top of Activity Details, you’ll see: Activity title Date and time Device used to record the activity Next to the Headline title, tap Edit to add notes or a reflection about your workout. View the Activity Map For supported activities with route data: Tap View on the map. Drag your finger along the route to review data from different points in the workout. Tap the current metric to change the data displayed. Available map metrics depend on the activity and recorded data. To change the map style: Tap the map icon in the top-right corner. Select Default or Satellite. Tap the X in the top-left corner to close the map. Activity Options Tap the three dots (...) in the top-right corner to access the following options: Sync to Strava Unlink Activity, if the workout is linked to another activity Save as New Routine Edit Log Details Delete Edit Cardio Activity Details Tap the three dots (...) in the top-right corner. Tap Edit Log Details. Update any available details, including: Title Date Duration Active Energy Distance Elevation Perceived Effort Reflection Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes. Available fields depend on the activity type. Customize Cardio Activity Charts Under Workout Details, tap Charts to view and customize the charts available for the activity. Scrub through a chart to view data at a specific point in time. Charts may include: General: Elevation and Heart Rate Cycling: Speed, Cadence, and Power Running: Pace, Cadence, Stride Length, Vertical Oscillation, and Ground Contact Time Swimming: Pace and Cadence Rowing: Pace To reorder your charts: Tap the reorder icon in the top-right corner. Press and hold a chart, then drag it to your desired position. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save. Tap the X in the top-left corner to exit. Personal Records Any Personal Records you set during this activity will appear here. Activity Strain Activity Strain shows how much the workout contributed to your total daily Strain: 0: No added Strain 1–20: Little physical demand 21–40: Slightly increased your daily Strain 41–60: Added noticeable Strain 61–80: Made up a major part of today’s Strain 80+: Added a very high load from this activity Cardio Workout Metrics The metrics shown under Workout Details depend on the activity: Activity Available metrics Running Active Duration, Total Duration, Distance, Avg Pace, Avg Cadence, Avg HR, Avg Power, Active Energy, Total Energy Cycling Active Duration, Total Duration, Distance, Avg Speed, Avg Cadence, Avg HR, Elevation Gain, Avg Power, Active Energy, Total Energy Pool Swim Active Duration, Total Duration, Distance, Avg Pace, Avg HR, Laps, Pool Length, Active Energy, Total Energy Open-Water Swim Active Duration, Total Duration, Distance, Avg Pace, Cadence, Avg HR, Active Energy, Total Energy Multisport Active Duration, Total Duration, Distance, Avg HR, Active Energy, Total Energy Other Activity Active Duration, Total Duration, Avg HR, Active Energy, Total Energy Tap All Metrics to view the complete list of available workout data. Perceived Effort Perceived Effort reflects how difficult the workout felt: 1: Minimal Effort 2: Very Light 3: Light 4: Moderate 5: Tiring 6: Somewhat Hard 7: Hard 8: Very Hard 9: Extremely Hard 10: Maximum Effort You can update Perceived Effort through Edit Log Details. Heart The Heart section may include: Cardio Load: The workout’s contribution to your cardiovascular training load Heart Rate: Your average, minimum, and maximum heart rate, along with a chart Heart Rate Zones: The percentage of the workout and heart rate range spent in each zone Cardio Focus: Your percentage and Cardio Points across Low Aerobic, High Aerobic, and Anaerobic training Heart Rate Recovery: The difference between your peak exercise heart rate and your heart rate during recovery Cardio Load statuses include Calibrating, Detraining, Maintaining, Productive, Peaking, Fatigued, and Overtraining. Muscles When available, the Muscles section includes: Muscular Load Muscle Breakdown Tap See More under Muscle Breakdown to view all the muscle groups used during the workout. Intervals For supported workouts, the Intervals section displays a breakdown of each recorded interval. Running Intervals Interval Duration Distance Avg Pace Avg HR Avg Power Avg Cadence Elevation Gain Cycling Intervals Interval Duration Distance Avg Speed Avg HR Avg Power Avg Cadence Elevation Gain Scroll horizontally to view all available interval metrics. Splits For supported activities, the Splits section divides your workout into selected distance increments. Tap the current split distance to select a different increment. Activity Available split distances Indoor Run 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Outdoor Run 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Track and Field 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Wheelchair Run Pace 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Hiking 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Indoor Walk 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Outdoor Walk 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Wheelchair Walk Pace 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Indoor Cycle 5, 10, or 20 mi/km Outdoor Cycle 5, 10, or 20 mi/km Hand Cycling 5, 10, or 20 mi/km Elliptical 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Open-Water Swim 100, 500, or 1,000 yd/m Pool Swim 100, 500, or 1,000 yd/m Paddle Sports 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Rowing 500 m, 1 km, or 2 km Sailing 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Cross-Country Skiing 1, 5, or 10 mi/km Skating Sports 1, 5, or 10 mi/km The Splits table may include: Distance Duration Speed or Pace Heart Rate Power Cadence Elevation Gain Scroll horizontally to view all available metrics. Tap the arrow next to Splits to open the complete table.
