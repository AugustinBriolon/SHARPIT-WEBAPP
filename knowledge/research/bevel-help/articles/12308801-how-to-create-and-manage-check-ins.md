# How to Create and Manage Check-ins

**Category:** Bevel Intelligence  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/12308801

You can easily create and manage check-ins in Bevel Intelligence. Option 1: Create a Check-in with Bevel Intelligence Open Bevel Intelligence. Ask Bevel Intelligence exactly what you want to track, including: the type of information you want included, how often you want the check-in, and the time you want to receive it. Example: “Send me a daily recovery summary every morning at 8am.” Option 2: Create and Manage Check-ins Manually Open Bevel Intelligence. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner. Tap Check-ins. Create a Check-in Tap the + button in the top-right corner. Enter: Title Instructions: additional context or instructions for Bevel Intelligence Schedule, including: Frequency (once, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly) Days Time End Date Tap Save to create the check-in. Edit a Check-in Open the Check-ins page. Tap the check-in you want to edit. Update any details you’d like to change. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes. Pause or Delete a Check-in To temporarily turn off a check-in, toggle it off. To permanently delete a check-in: Open the check-in. Tap Delete Check-in.
