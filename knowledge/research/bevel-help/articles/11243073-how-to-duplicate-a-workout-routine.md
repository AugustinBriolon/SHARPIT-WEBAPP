# How to Duplicate a Workout Routine

**Category:** Strength Builder  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11243073

Duplicating a Routine allows you to create a copy of an existing workout so you can modify it without changing the original. Steps to Duplicate a Routine Open Bevel. Go to the Fitness tab. Scroll down to Routines. Tap the three dots (...) next to the Strength Routine you want to duplicate. Select Duplicate. Tip: Once duplicated, you can edit the new Routine to adjust exercises, sets, reps, or timers without affecting the original.
