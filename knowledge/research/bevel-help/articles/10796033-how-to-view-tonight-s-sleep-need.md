# How to View Tonight’s Sleep Need

**Category:** Sleep  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10796033

Bevel helps you see how much Sleep you need. You can read more about Sleep Needed here. How to View Tonight’s Sleep Needed Open Bevel. Tap Sleep Score Ring. Scroll down and tap Tonight’s Sleep Needed to view the estimate of how much Sleep you need tonight. Tip: If you enter your wake-up time above Tonight’s Sleep Needed, Bevel will use your Sleep Needed to estimate your wind-down time and target bedtime for the night.
