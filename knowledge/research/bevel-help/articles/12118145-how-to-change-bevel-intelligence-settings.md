# How to Change Bevel Intelligence Settings

**Category:** Bevel Intelligence  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/12118145

You can update your Bevel Intelligence settings at any time. In Bevel Intelligence settings, you can manage: Turning Bevel Intelligence on or off Choosing your Bevel Intelligence personality Viewing your usage, including your weekly limit, how much you have used, and when your limit resets Managing and purchasing additional credits Turning expanded thinking steps on or off, which shows the reasoning steps for each response by default Turning suggested follow-ups on or off, which shows quick follow-up questions after each response Connecting and managing Calendars Ghost Mode: Persistent Knowledge, Chat History, Default to Ghost Mode Reproductive Health: Allow Bevel Intelligence to understand your cycle, upcoming periods, and offer personalized insights. Advanced: Model Selection (Standard, Upgraded), Beta Features Re-syncing chat history Deleting all chats Deleting all files Deleting all data Option 1: Via Profile Settings Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Bevel Intelligence. Select the setting you want to update. Option 2: Via Bevel Intelligence Open Bevel Intelligence. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner. Tap Settings in the bottom right corner. Select the setting you want to update.
