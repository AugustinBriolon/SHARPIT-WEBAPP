# How to Pin Journal Tags

**Category:** Journal  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/13319169

Pinned tags appear at the top of your daily Journal, making them easier to access. Steps to Pin Journal Option 1: Via Pinned Tags Open Bevel. Tap the Journal tab. Tap the three dots (...) in the top-right corner. Tap Pinned Tags. Tap the Pin icon next to a tag. A filled black pin indicates the tag is pinned. Tap the pin again to unpin the tag. Tap Save to apply your changes. Option 2: From the Journal Tab Open Bevel. Tap the Journal tab. Select the desired journal entry. If available, tap the Settings icon in the top-right corner. Tap the Pin icon. Tap Save. Option 3: Via Customize Journal Open Bevel. Tap the Journal tab. Tap the three dots (...) in the top-right corner. Select Customize Journal. Tap the Settings icon next to the desired journal entry. Tap the Pin icon. Tap Save.
