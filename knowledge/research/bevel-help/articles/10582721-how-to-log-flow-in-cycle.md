# How to Log Flow in Cycle

**Category:** Cycle  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10582721

Logging your flow helps Bevel better understand your cycle patterns and improve period and ovulation predictions. Steps to Log Flow Option 1: Open Bevel. Tap Current Cycle under the Cycle section. Tap the + button below and to the right of today’s date. Choose your flow level (No Flow, Light, Medium, Heavy). Tap Add to Log. Option 2: Open Bevel. Tap Current Cycle under the Cycle section. Tap underneath today’s date. Tap + Flow in the bottom-right corner. Choose your flow level (No Flow, Light, Medium, Heavy). Tap Add to Log.
