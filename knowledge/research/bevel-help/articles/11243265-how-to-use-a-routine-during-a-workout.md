# How to Use a Routine During a Workout

**Category:** Strength Builder  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11243265

Bevel lets you make changes to your workout even after you’ve started, so you can adjust exercises, sets, reps, timers, or supersets as needed. From Your Phone Start your workout: Open Bevel. Go to the Fitness tab. Scroll down to Routines. Select the Routine you want to start. Tap Start. Note: If the routine is scheduled for today, it will also appear under Today’s Activity, where you can select it to start your workout. Begin a set: Press the Play button to start tracking the first set. Complete a set: Press the Checkmark to complete a set. Edit exercises or settings at any time: Tap the three dots (...) next to the workout or an exercise to: Replace an exercise Delete an exercise Edit a superset or circuit Tap the clock icon to add or adjust a timer. Tap the time between sets to adjust rest periods. Tap the numbers in the columns to adjust weight, reps, or sets. Additional options during a workout: Add new exercises at the bottom of the workout. Reorder exercises by tapping the three dots (...) at the top and selecting Reorder Exercises. Pause the workout by tapping the three dots (...) at the top and selecting Pause Workout. Swap between Reps and Duration by tapping the reps or duration value for an exercise, then tapping the toggle above the keyboard to switch between the two. From Your Apple Watch Start your workout: Open the Bevel app on your watch. Tap the notepad icon in the top-right corner. Select to start a workout from Today’s Activity or the Routine you want to do. Tap Start. Follow the on-screen prompts to track your exercises, sets, reps, and timers. Begin a set: Press the Play button to start tracking the first set. Complete a set: Press the Checkmark. Then press Save. Bevel will automatically move you to the next exercise. Note: You can skip the set-by-set confirmation in advanced settings.  You can start and stop a set on the watch in multiple ways: Tap the play and checkmark buttons in the app. Use the double-tap gesture on the hand wearing your watch. Press the Action button. Note: How do I set up the Action button to start/stop a set on the watch? On your watch, go to Settings → Action Button, then set it to Workouts and select the Bevel app. This only works for Apple Watch Ultra 2 and above. Note: How do I set up Gestures to start/stop a set on the watch? On your watch, go to Settings → Gestures and toggle it on. This only works for Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 2, and above. Preview an Exercise While viewing a workout on your Apple Watch, tap an exercise title to view an exercise preview with instructions and a demonstration video. Edit Exercises While viewing a workout on your Apple Watch, you can edit the exercises or settings at any time. Tap the current exercise set to quickly edit its weight and reps. Scroll down and tap the pencil icon next to an exercise to edit its weight, reps, or sets, delete the exercise, or scroll to the bottom to add another exercise. Pause or End a Workout on Apple Watch While a workout is in progress: Swipe left on the workout screen. Tap Pause icon to pause your workout. To resume, tap Start icon. To end your workout, touch and hold the X button. Tip: After finishing a workout, you can save your updated weights to the Routine for future sessions. For step-by-step guidance, please refer to this article.
