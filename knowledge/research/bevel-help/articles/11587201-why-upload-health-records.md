# Why Upload Health Records?

**Category:** Health Records  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11587201

Bevel lets you upload Health Records so all your important medical information is in one secure place. This ensures Bevel reflects your true health status, helping you make smarter decisions about your wellness. Please note that Bevel is not a medical provider and does not offer medical advice. Types of Health Records You can upload a wide range of documents. Examples include, but are not limited to, the following. Lab Reports: Results from blood work or other lab tests that provide insight into biomarkers like cholesterol, glucose, hormones, and more. We also extract key blood biomarkers from these reports and display them in Biology. Medications: Records of prescribed or over-the-counter drugs, including dosage and frequency, used to manage conditions or support health. Imaging Reports: Summaries from scans like X-rays, MRIs, or CT scans that help diagnose or monitor medical conditions. Immunization Records: A history of vaccines received, including dates and types, used to track protection against diseases. Clinical Reports: Notes and summaries from healthcare providers that document diagnoses, treatments, and overall care plans. Intake Forms: Information collected during healthcare visits, such as medical history, symptoms, and personal details provided before or during an appointment. Administrative Documents: Non-clinical records like billing information, insurance details, and consent forms related to your care. Personal Health Reports: Self-reported or compiled health data, such as wellness summaries, lifestyle tracking, or reports generated from apps and devices. Why Uploading Helps Accurate Insights: Bevel uses your records to provide a complete and reliable view of your health. Biological Age & Wellness Metrics: Bevel analyzes your blood biomarker data to estimate Biological Age and highlight Trends that impact long-term health. Trend Tracking: Maintain a continuous history of labs, tests, and visits to spot patterns over time. Preventive & Personalized Guidance: AI-driven insights and recommendations to support recovery, manage risks, and improve overall wellness.
