# How to Create Recipes

**Category:** Nutrition  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11250497

You can create a Recipe by combining multiple ingredients into a single meal and automatically calculating its nutritional value. This is ideal for homemade dishes, meal prep, or recipes that include a mix of packaged and fresh foods. Option 1 (Via My Foods): Navigate to My Foods. Tap + Add in the top-right corner. Tap Create Recipe. Choose how to add ingredients: Import Recipe: Write or paste recipe details, capture with your camera, or import a photo. Scan Barcode: Scan single or multiple barcodes (use Multi-scan for multiple items). Search Food: Select ingredients from Bevel’s food database. Option 2 (Via Timeline): Open Bevel. Scroll down and tap View Timeline. Make sure all the food items you want in the recipe are logged. Press and hold one of the food items. Tap Multi-Select. Select all the food items you want to include. Tap Create Recipe.
