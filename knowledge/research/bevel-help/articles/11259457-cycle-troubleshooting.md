# Cycle Troubleshooting

**Category:** Cycle  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11259457

Why is my Cycle Data stuck during Onboarding? If you’ve previously used other apps or devices to track your cycle, multiple data sources may be sending information to Bevel. Here’s how to resolve it. Step 1: Adjust Your Cycle Data Sources in Bevel To make sure Bevel only receives cycle data from the correct sources: Open Bevel Go to Profile Select Data Sources Tap Cycle Choose Hide Data Sources Add any other cycle-tracking apps to your hidden sources, leaving only Bevel and Apple Health active This ensures Bevel uses clean, accurate data for your cycle tracking. Step 2: Restart the Onboarding Process Once your data sources are updated, please go through the onboarding flow again. Step 3: Send Us Your Logs (Optional but Helpful!) To help us diagnose and resolve the issue faster, we’d greatly appreciate your logs: Open Bevel → Profile Scroll down and tap “Send logs to developer” Then email us at hello@bevel.health with the name or email you use to log in to Bevel Your logs help us identify what’s causing the issue and improve the experience for everyone.
