# How to Report a Nutrition Issue

**Category:** Nutrition  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11247937

Our team works hard to review foods and fix any issues in the database. If you notice a problem with a food item, you can submit it for review. The more information you provide, the faster and more accurately we can address it. Option 1: Report via Food Logging When logging food: Find the food in the database that has the issue. Tap Report Issue at the bottom of the screen.  Fill out the required fields and submit. Option 2: Report via Nutrition Section Open Bevel. Scroll down to the Nutrition section and tap to open it. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Tap Report Food. Fill out the required fields and submit. Option 3: Report via Timeline Open Bevel. Scroll down to the Timeline section. Tap the food item you wish to report. Tap the three dots (…) in the top-right corner. Select Edit Log Details. Tap Report Issue at the bottom of the screen.  Fill out the required fields and submit.
