# How to Change Units of Measurement

**Category:** App Customization  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10430913

Bevel lets you choose the units of measurement that best suit your preferences. You can change units for weight, distance, temperature, and other tracked metrics to make your data easier to read and use. This article explains how to update your units of measurement in Bevel. Accessing Unit Settings To change units: Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Unit of Measurement From here, you can update the units used throughout Bevel. Changing Units Bevel allows you to switch between common units: Distance: kilometers (km) or miles (mi) Height: feet (ft) or meters (m) Weight: kilograms (kg) or pounds (lb) Temperature: Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (°F) Energy: calories (kcal) or kilojoules (kJ) Water: fluid ounces (fl oz) or milliliters (mL) Glucose: milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL) or millimoles per liter (mmol/L) To update a unit: Tap the metric you want to change. Select your preferred unit from the available options. Tap Done. Tap Save in the top-right corner to apply your changes.
