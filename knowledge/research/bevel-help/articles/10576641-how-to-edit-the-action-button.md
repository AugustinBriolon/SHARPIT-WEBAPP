# How to Edit the Action Button

**Category:** App Customization  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/10576641

The Action Button is a quick and easy way to customize and access the items you use most. It’s the “+” button located on the bottom-right of the screen. Edit the Action Button Open Bevel. Tap the + button in the bottom-right corner. Press and hold on any icon to enter edit mode. Tap Add in the top left to choose and add a new action. Tap the - button on any existing action to remove it. Tap Save to confirm your changes. Action Button Options Log Food: A quick way to track meals. Choose from: Scan Food: Barcode scan Capture Food: Take a photo Describe Food: Describe what you ate Search Food: Manual database search Import Food: From your photo library Log Activity: To manually log a workout or other movement View Routines: Open your saved workout Routines and start one instantly Generating Routines: Build a new workout Routine Ask Bevel: Ask Bevel Intelligence a question Add Hydration: Log your water intake Log Nap: Record a nap Add Caffeine: Track caffeine intake Log Cycle: Track your period and symptoms
