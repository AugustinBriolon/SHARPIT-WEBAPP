# How to Customize Strength Builder Settings

**Category:** Strength Builder  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11213505

Bevel’s Strength Builder allows you to customize your strength training sessions to fit your goals, track progress, and optimize your workouts. These customizations allow you to personalize your workouts for your pace and goals, making each session more effective. Live Strength Builder Customization Settings Default Rest Time: The rest time used when enabled for an exercise. Interval Haptic: Set haptic frequency for intervals during workouts. Interval Haptic Time: Set the interval time. Set-By-Set Confirmation: Review and confirm each set before proceeding to the next one. Prioritize Plate Calculator: For bar-based exercises, the weight field opens the Plate Calculator first. Note: this works only in live mode and not while editing a workout before it starts. Auto-Fill Sets: Automatically fills remaining sets with values from your last completed set.  Auto-Update Routine: Automatically saves the changes you make during a workout back to its Routine when you finish. This includes updates to weights, reps, sets, exercise order, and any exercises you replace or add, so the completed workout becomes your new Routine. Skipped exercises are not removed from the Routine. Share Watch Motion Data: Help improve Strength Builder by sharing accelerometer data with Bevel. No workout or personal details will be shared. Routine Preferences Customization Settings Experience: Your comfort with exercises and routines. Goal: The results you’re working toward, sharing your exercises, effort, and progress. Equipment: The equipment available to you. Structure: Choose from Warm Up, Failure, Cool Down, Dropset, and Supersets/Circuits. Excluded Exercises: Exercises to not include when creating Routines. Sync Cardio to Apple Fitness Select Sync to Apple Fitness to keep your scheduled cardio workouts up to date in Apple Fitness. Scheduled Workouts Sync Select Sync Scheduled Workouts to refresh your scheduled workouts with the latest changes. How to Edit Customization Settings Option 1: Via Profile Open Bevel. Tap your initials or profile picture in the top-right corner. Tap Customization. Tap Fitness. Choose the settings you want to change: Live Strength Builder to adjust Strength Builder settings. Routine Preferences to customize your routines. Option 2: Via Fitness Tab Open Bevel. Go to the Fitness tab. Tap the three dots (...) in the top-right corner Tap Fitness Settings. Choose the settings you want to change: Live Strength Builder to adjust Strength Builder settings. Routine Preferences to customize your routines.
