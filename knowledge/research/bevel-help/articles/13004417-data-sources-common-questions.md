# Data Sources Common Questions

**Category:** Data Sources  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/13004417

We’ve compiled answers to the most frequently asked questions about Data Sources. Can I use Bevel's Smart Alarm with Fitbit or without an Apple Watch? Not at this time. Currently, Bevel's Smart Alarm requires an Apple Watch to function. If you don't have an Apple Watch, Smart Alarm is not supported on Fitbit or other devices. Why isn't my nutrition data syncing from Google Health to Bevel? Nutrition data from Google Health does not currently sync to Bevel. How do I see live heart rate data from my Polar H10 during a Strength Builder workout? To see your heart rate in real time during a Strength Builder workout, connect your Polar H10 directly to your iPhone via Bluetooth before starting the workout in Bevel. If you instead connect the H10 through Polar Beat, your heart rate data is only written to Apple Health after you end the workout in Polar Beat. As a result, Bevel will sync the data after the workout has finished, rather than displaying it live during the session. For real-time heart rate in Bevel: Pair your Polar H10 directly with your iPhone using Bluetooth. Open Bevel and start your Strength Builder workout. Your heart rate should stream live from Apple Health into Bevel during the workout. How do I see live heart rate data from my Garmin watch? Live heart rate data from Garmin watches is not currently supported in Bevel. What does Nutrients in Data Sources do? Nutrients in Data Sources controls where your nutrient amounts come from, not your food logs or Nutrition Score. This means you can use a different data source for nutrients while still receiving a Nutrition Score in Bevel.
