# How to Use Journal

**Category:** Journal  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/13318977

Below is an explanation of how to use the Journal feature. Your entries today will contribute to the 90-day rolling data for tomorrow’s insights. View today's Journal You can view today's Journal by following these steps: Open Bevel. Tap the Journal tab. You'll see three sections: Daytime Entries Nighttime Entries Automatic Entries Log a Journal entry There are three types of Journal entries: Automatic: Recorded automatically from supported data sources. Manual: Logged using Yes, Neutral, or No. Value-based: Logged by entering a value or selecting from a scale, such as your mood. Log a Yes/Neutral/No entry Each manual Journal entry has three possible states: ✔ Checkmark: Yes, this happened. ➖ Minus: Neutral / No effect. ✕ X: No, this did not happen. Tap the state you want for each entry. Log a value-based entry Some Journal entries require you to enter a value instead of selecting Yes, Neutral, or No. Tap the entry and provide the requested value. To delete a value, tap the entry, then tap the red trash icon. To update a value, tap the entry, edit the amount or time, and then tap Update. Examples include: Mood Caffeine Water Vitamins Supplements Copy yesterday's entries You can quickly copy your Journal entries from the previous day. Scroll to the bottom of the Journal tab. Tap Copy entries from yesterday. Only toggle-based entries are copied. Text entries and other input types are not included. How Data is Logged: Automatic vs Manual Tracking Many metrics are automatically synced from Apple Health. For all of the metrics below (except Target Strain Overreached), you can customize the threshold that determines when the Journal entry is logged. For example, you can change the 10,000 Steps threshold to 6,000 Steps.
