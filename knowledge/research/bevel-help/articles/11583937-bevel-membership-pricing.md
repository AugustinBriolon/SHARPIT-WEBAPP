# Bevel Membership & Pricing

**Category:** Bevel Basics  
**Source:** https://help.bevel.health/en/articles/11583937

Bevel offers two ways to use the app: Free and Pro. Free gives you the full Bevel tracking experience, including Recovery, Sleep, Strain, Stress, Nutrition Tracking, Strength, Energy Bank, Cycle Tracking, Fitness Tracking, Health Monitors, and Journal. Pro is for members who want more out of their data. If you've gone beyond just wanting numbers and want a coach that helps you understand what those numbers mean, and guides you toward your goals, Pro unlocks Bevel Intelligence, Health Records, Biological Age, and Training Plans. Billing, Renewals, and Cancellation How billing works All Bevel memberships are processed through your Apple ID via the App Store. You will not be billed directly by Bevel. Pro (Monthly) renews every month on the date you first subscribed. Pro (Annual) renews every 12 months on the date you first subscribed. You can see your exact renewal date and manage your subscription anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. How to cancel You can cancel anytime from your Apple ID subscription settings: Open Settings on your iPhone Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions Tap Bevel, then Cancel Subscription If you cancel, you'll keep access to your paid features until the end of the current billing period. Switching plans You can switch between Pro (Monthly) and Pro (Annual) at any time in your Apple Subscription settings. Apple will prorate the change automatically. Refunds All Bevel refunds are handled directly by Apple, not by Bevel. Because your subscription is billed through the App Store, Bevel cannot issue refunds on Apple's behalf. To request a refund: Go to reportaproblem.apple.com Sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase Find your Bevel subscription charge and tap Report a Problem Choose the reason and submit your request Apple will review and respond directly, typically within 24 to 48 hours. Refund decisions are at Apple's discretion, and Apple's policies apply. If you're having an issue with the Bevel app itself (bugs, billing questions, or trouble with features), please reach out to us at hello@bevel.health before requesting a refund. We'd love the chance to help. Frequently Asked Questions Can I use Bevel for free forever? Yes. The Free plan is free forever, and any feature included in Free today will always remain free. We will never move an existing Free feature behind a paywall. New features we build in the future may be added to Pro, but the core Bevel experience you get on Free stays free, including AI food logging and AI workout generation. What happens to my data if I downgrade or cancel? All of your historical data, logs, and trends stay in your account. You just lose access to Pro-only features (Bevel Intelligence, Health Records, and Biological Age). I was already on a Pro plan. Will my price go up? No. Existing Pro subscribers are grandfathered into their original pricing. Here's how it works: Pro (Annual) subscribers keep their original price in perpetuity. As long as your annual subscription remains continuously active, your preserved price is honored at every renewal. Apple will not charge you the new price. Pro (Monthly) subscribers keep their original price through October 31, 2026. Monthly renewals on or before October 31, 2026 stay at your preserved price. Starting with your first renewal after that date, you'll move to the current Pro (Monthly) price. 60-day grace period if you cancel. If your subscription lapses (for example, a payment fails or you cancel), you have 60 days to resubscribe at your preserved price. After 60 days, resubscribing means paying the current Pro price. Switching plans. Switching between Pro (Monthly) and Pro (Annual) may move you to current pricing for the new plan, since each plan has its own price. If you're unsure, check the price shown in the App Store upgrade screen before confirming. Notifications from Apple. If pricing ever changes in a way that affects you, Apple will notify you directly by email, push notification, and in-app message ahead of your next renewal. You can check your current price anytime under Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Bevel. Can I share my subscription with family?
